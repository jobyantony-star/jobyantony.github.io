Act I — The Performer

About Joby

01 / 06

From village stage programmes to the big screen — a journey built one performance at a time.

Joby Konni's story begins where most Kerala performers' stories begin: on a stage, in front of a live audience that forgives nothing and rewards everything. Years of mimicry shows and programme coordination sharpened an instinct for timing, character, and audience — instincts that carried directly onto film sets in Theekkuchiyum Panithulliyum, Ave Maria, Prekashante Metro, and Kalam Standard 5B.

Alongside acting, Joby edits his own visual work, runs a growing vlog, and founded Konni Varthakal — a local media platform built to give his community's stories a home online. Every role feeds the others: the stage informs the screen, the screen informs the edit, and the edit informs the newsroom.
