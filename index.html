01 — About

A performer at home
in front of any lens.

Joby Konni is a Malayalam film actor, vlogger, and media personality whose work moves fluidly between cinema, stage, and screen. Since his debut in Theekkuchiyum Panithulliyum (2016), he has built a body of work defined by presence, timing, and an instinct for the moment that makes an audience lean in.

Beyond the camera, Joby is the founder and admin of Konni Varthakal, a media platform that has become a trusted voice for stories from Konni and beyond — blending journalism, entertainment, and community in equal measure.
